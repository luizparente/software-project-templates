# User Roles and Personas

## Overview

This document defines all distinct user roles and personas that will interact with your product. User roles represent different categories of users, each with unique needs, goals, and capabilities. Personas are detailed characterizations of specific user types that make these roles concrete and memorable. Clearly defining roles and personas ensures consistency across your user stories and helps your team maintain a shared understanding of who they are building for.

**What we expect to see:** Create a comprehensive section for each user role or persona in your system. For each role, provide a name, a brief description of who this user is, their primary goals and motivations, their level of technical expertise, and any relevant constraints or characteristics. Every user role referenced in your user stories (via the User/Role field) must be defined in this document. Aim for clarity and specificity that will help developers, designers, and testers understand exactly who is using each feature.
